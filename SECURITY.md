# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest release of Ragnar's Den gets fixes. The live app at <https://xrafaelx.github.io/Ragnar-s-Den/> always runs it, and installed copies update themselves.

## Reporting a problem

Please don't report a security problem in a public issue. Use the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability** instead. Only the maintainers can see what you send there.

Please include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and which browser and device you used. You'll get a reply as soon as possible.

## What the app does with your data

Ragnar's Den runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no accounts and no tracking. Your characters and homebrew are kept in your browser's storage and only leave your device when you export a backup yourself. See the [privacy policy](https://xrafaelx.github.io/Ragnar-s-Den/privacy.html) for details.
