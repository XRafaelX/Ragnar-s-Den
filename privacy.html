Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Short version: Ragnar's Den keeps everything on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing you create is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores

Your characters, homebrew, encounters and settings (such as your theme) are saved in your browser's local storage, on the device you use. That data stays there. We cannot see it, and we do not have a server that receives it.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-ins.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no cookies.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties.

Backups and sharing

The Import & Export button saves your data to a file on your device, or reads one back. The file only goes where you choose to put it. If you share it, that is your choice.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs, such as your IP address and the time of a visit, when you load the site. That is outside the app and subject to GitHub's privacy statement. After the first visit the app works offline and makes no further requests of its own.

Deleting your data

Clearing this site's data in your browser (or uninstalling the app) deletes everything the app has saved, and it cannot be recovered. Use Import & Export to keep a backup first.

Children

The app is a general tabletop game tool. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/XRafaelX/Ragnar-s-Den/issues.

Ragnar's Den is an unofficial fan tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
